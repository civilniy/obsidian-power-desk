Obsidian Power Desk

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Obsidian Power Desk handles information when a user connects a Google account.

Information accessed

The application may access Google Calendar information that the user authorizes, including calendars, events, event times, titles, descriptions, and related calendar metadata.

How information is used

Calendar information is used only to provide calendar and planning features requested by the user, such as viewing, creating, updating, or deleting calendar events.

Storage and sharing

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. Authentication tokens and calendar data are handled only as needed to provide the requested functionality.

User control

A user can revoke the application's access at any time from the security settings of their Google Account. Revoking access stops future access to Google Calendar data.

Google API Services

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the project repository.
